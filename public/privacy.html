Standup Tracker

Privacy Policy

Effective date: March 17, 2026

Standup Tracker is an experimental, open-source Google Meet add-on. We are not a business, we do not monetize this project, and we have no interest in collecting or profiting from your data.

What We Collect

Standup Tracker processes the minimum data needed to function:

  • Meeting identifiers — A Google Meet meeting code is used to create a shared session so participants can see the same checklist.
  • Participant display names — Names shown in Google Meet are fetched via the Google Meet API to populate the checklist. These are the same names visible to all meeting participants.
  • Checklist state — Which participants have been marked as "done" during a standup session.

What We Do NOT Collect

  • Email addresses
  • Audio, video, or meeting content
  • Google account credentials or tokens from end users
  • Analytics, telemetry, or usage tracking
  • Cookies or advertising identifiers

How Data Is Stored

Session data (meeting ID, participant names, checklist state) is stored in a Supabase-hosted PostgreSQL database for the duration of the meeting session. This data is not associated with any user account and is not used for any purpose beyond powering the real-time checklist.

Data is retained only as long as needed for the session to function. We do not perform long-term storage, archiving, or analysis of meeting data.

Third-Party Services

Standup Tracker uses the following third-party services to operate:

  • Google Meet API — To retrieve participant names for the active meeting.
  • Supabase — For real-time database storage and synchronization.
  • Vercel — For hosting the application.

We do not share your data with any other third parties, advertisers, or data brokers.

Data Security

All communication between the add-on and our servers occurs over HTTPS. Access to the backend infrastructure is restricted to the project maintainers. However, as an experimental project provided as-is, we cannot guarantee the security of any data processed by this application. Please see our Terms of Service for important disclaimers.

Children's Privacy

Standup Tracker is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected on this page with an updated effective date. Continued use of the add-on after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, you can open an issue on our GitHub repository.
