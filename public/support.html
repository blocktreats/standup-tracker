CapyUp

Support

CapyUp is a lightweight tool built for teams who want a simple way to track standups in Google Meet. It's not a heavily maintained commercial product, but we'll do our best to help if you run into issues.

Help

Here's how to get started with CapyUp:

  1. Install the add-on — your Google Workspace admin installs CapyUp from the Marketplace link provided
  2. Connect your org — one person from your organization visits the setup page and connects their Google account
  3. Open in Meet — in any Google Meet call, click the Activities icon (puzzle piece) and select CapyUp
  4. Track your standup — participants are auto-detected. Tap names to mark them as done. Everyone with the add-on open sees the same list in real time.

Common questions:

  • Participants aren't showing up? The person who connected their account on the setup page must be present in the meeting. This is a Google API limitation.
  • Can everyone see the checklist? Yes — anyone who opens the add-on in the same meeting sees the same list, synced in real time.
  • Do all participants need to install it? No. Only the people who want to view or manage the checklist need to open it. Participants are tracked whether or not they have the add-on open.
  • Can I add people manually? Yes — use the text input at the top of the add-on to add anyone not auto-detected.
