WorkUp

Standup tracker for Google Meet

Connect your Google Workspace

One person from your organization needs to connect their Google account. This lets WorkUp automatically see who's in your meetings.

What this does

Grants read-only access to meeting participant names. WorkUp cannot join, record, or modify your meetings. The connected account must be present in a meeting for participant auto-fetch to work.

Only a Google Workspace admin or regular user needs to do this once per organization.

You're connected!

Your organization has been linked to WorkUp.

Next steps

  1. Install the WorkUp add-on from your Google Workspace admin panel
  2. Open Google Meet and start or join a meeting
  3. Click the Activities icon (puzzle piece) and open WorkUp
  4. Participant names will auto-populate — tap to mark done
